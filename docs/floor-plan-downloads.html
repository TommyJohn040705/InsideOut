UW floor plans to download for InsideOut

Sign in to plantops.uwaterloo.ca in this browser first (UW login). Then click each link to download it. Tick the box as you go.
Skip the restricted ones unless Plant Operations has given permission.
When done, put the PDFs (keep their names) in data-sources/plantops/pdfs/ of the InsideOut folder, or add them in the app: campus map → Floor plans → Bulk import.
